# Rutina – by Nature's Finest

The app was renamed from FinestMe to **Rutina** (October 2026).

## Logo
- **Symbol:** an open loop (the daily round, a streak ring) that sprouts the Nature's Finest leaf at its open end. It stands for small steps repeated every day, rooted in nature.
- **Where:** the symbol is used **only as the app icon** (`icons/icon.svg`). Inside the app, on share cards and in communication the brand is the **wordmark alone** (`icons/rutina-logo.svg`, `brandMark()` in `js/app.js`, `js/share.js`).
- **Wordmark:** "rutina" in lowercase, **Fraunces** Bold (SOFT 100, optical size 144; SIL Open Font License, free for logos), tracking −0.03em, with the endorsement "BY NATURE'S FINEST" in Rethink Sans Medium, set to the same width underneath. For the trademark filing and print, convert the wordmark to outlines.
- **Colours:** ink #0f1b14 (light) / #eef4ef (dark); leaf #0ba74b (brand leaf green) or #3ec46d on dark.
- **Clear space:** the height of the letter "r" around the wordmark. Minimum app-icon size: 24 px.

## Trademark (to do before launch – legal)
- "Rutina" is an everyday word in sl, hr, cs, sk, es, pl ("rutyna") and similar languages, and it describes what the app does. A **word mark** at EUIPO is therefore likely to be refused as descriptive or lacking distinctiveness (Art. 7(1)(b),(c) EUTMR).
- **Recommended filing:** a **figurative EU mark** (symbol + "rutina" + "by Nature's Finest"), classes 9 (downloadable app), 42 (SaaS), 44 (health and wellness advice). Optionally add 5 (supplements), with the Nature's Finest umbrella.
- **Before filing:** run a clearance search in EUIPO eSearch and TMview for "RUTINA" / "RUTINE" / "ROUTINA" in classes 9, 42 and 44, and check the App Store and Google Play for apps with the same name.
- **Fallback if clearance fails:** keep the symbol and use a coined spelling that stays readable in all markets (for example "Rutyna") or "Nature's Finest Rutina".
- **Grammar:** in Slavic languages the name stays undeclined and capitalised as a brand ("Dodaj Rutina na začetni zaslon"). Reward codes (FINESTME-SHIP, FINESTME15) stay unchanged because they are live in the webshops.
